## The legend

When the iron fell, an old woman in the caravan had just finished a Sadu blanket. She had woven it for a grandchild who was still a long road away, and she had folded it on top of her satchel so it would be the first thing she packed in the morning.

A spark fell into the fold.

In the morning the blanket was sitting up, wrapped around itself, with the satchel strap across its shoulder. It looked at the old woman with the small dark face of the night sky, went very shy, and then held out the blanket's corner to her, because she looked cold.

That has been Keeper ever since: shy when you look at him, happiest when you're close, and always holding something out to someone.

## The satchel

Keeper carries other people's little things. Not treasure: the small things people can't carry themselves.

- **A date seed**, from the first palm someone planted in an oasis that is now a town.
- **A letter** that was never sent, because the person it was for came home before it could be.
- **The words of a song**: a *hida'*, one of the old camel-driving songs, that the caravan sang to keep the camels walking in step. Nobody alive remembers the tune. Keeper is still hoping someone will.

The satchel holds exactly as much as someone needs kept. It has never been full, and Keeper has never lost a single thing from it. Each thing in it, Keeper says, is being held *for* someone, and one day each one will be asked for. Keeper intends to be ready.

He also carries a rope. Nobody knows why. Keeper says *"just in case"*, and so far he has been right every time.

## Who he is

Keeper is the gentle one and the slow one, a little sleepy, the first to doze off when it goes quiet. When you look at him, he goes shy: he turns a little away, sways and blushes. When you pet him, he melts. When something goes wrong, he worries, mostly about you.

He fusses over everyone. He is the one asking *"Is everyone warm enough?"* at every campfire, and the one who brought the extra dates.

## Habits

- Yawns, tilts his head, twitches an ear (slowly, every 9 to 15 seconds).
- His face at rest is **content**.
- His smile is **bashful**: a tiny mouth and his deepest blush.

## With the others

- **Fennec** pretends she doesn't want the dates. Keeper puts one down next to her anyway and looks away.
- **Drifter** collects stories; Keeper collects things. They have agreed this makes them the camp's two librarians.
- **Scout** forgets to pack. Keeper packs for Scout.
