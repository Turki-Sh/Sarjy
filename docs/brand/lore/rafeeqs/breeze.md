## The legend

The old Arabs named their winds. **al-Saba** (الصبا), the east wind, was the gentle one, the one the poets loved, the one that brought the scent of home. **al-Dabur** (الدبور), the west wind, was the wild one.

Somewhere in the desert there is a place where al-Saba and al-Dabur meet and turn around each other. A spark fell there, in the age of the incense road, and got caught in the turn.

It never got out. Breeze is that turn: half the gentle wind, half the wild one, spinning on the spot, forever. That is why she can't sit still, and why she is sometimes the softest thing in the camp and sometimes the most trouble.

## The sand she leaves behind

Breeze always leaves a little sand behind. It's not mess, or not only mess. The sand is a pinch of every place Breeze has been, red from one desert, white from another, gold from the Empty Quarter. If you know your sands, you can always tell where Breeze has been by what she left on your rug.

Breeze also carries smells, which is how she brings news before anyone else: rain on the way, bread in the next camp, a storm two days out. When Breeze goes very still and sniffs, everyone else goes quiet too.

## Who she is

Breeze is playful, mischievous, and never still. Try to touch her and she slips away from your hand, giggling. When you pet her anyway, she giggles and bounces. When something goes wrong, she spins it off. She doesn't really sleep. She drifts.

## Habits

- Spins, hops, looks around (every 5 to 9 seconds).
- Always drifting, always a little off the ground.
- Her smile is **cheeky**: a wink and her tongue out.
- On a save, she does a loop of joy.

## With the others

- **Scout** and Breeze race to every dune. Breeze always wins.
- **Lantern**'s light is the one thing Breeze can't blow out. Breeze keeps trying, mostly to make Lantern laugh.
- When something has to be lifted, carried up, or brought back from somewhere nobody else can reach, it's Breeze.
