## The legend

On the Night of Iron, someone in the caravan had hung a hooded cloak from the top of a tent pole to dry. It was the highest point in the camp. A spark fell straight into the hood.

So Scout's very first moment in the world was looking down at everything from above: the ring of camels, the sleepers, the smoking craters, the whole dark Empty Quarter rolling away under the stars. He decided on the spot that this was the correct way to see things and has been trying to get higher ever since.

Every morning before sunrise, Scout climbs the tallest dune he can find to see what's coming. In all the years since, he has never once been surprised, and he would very much like to keep it that way.

## The scout's creed

Caravans used to send a *ra'id*, a scout, ahead of them to find water and grazing and come back with the truth. There is an old Arabic saying about it: **الرائد لا يكذب أهله**, *the scout does not lie to his people.*

Scout takes this extremely seriously. He reports exactly what he sees, no more and no less, even when what he sees is *"more sand."*

And he is always the first to see **Suhail** rise at the end of every summer. He waits up for him every year, on the highest dune, facing south.

## Who he is

Scout is curious and quick, all eyes and leaning in. When you look at him, he leans right in to look back, and his eyes follow you more keenly than anyone else's. He's ticklish when you pet him. When something goes wrong, he jumps. He rarely sleeps.

His flaw is the obvious one: he runs ahead. He gets to the top of the dune, shouts *"I'll climb up and look!"* over his shoulder, and is gone before anyone has agreed to anything.

## Habits

- Looks around, hops, tilts his head, looks around again (every 4 to 8 seconds).
- His face at rest is **wide**: all eyes, brows up.
- His smile is a **beam**, wide open.
- His arms flap when he's happy.

## With the others

- **Breeze** and Scout race to every dune. Breeze always wins. Scout always demands a rematch.
- **Rider** is the only one who can make Scout wait, with one word.
- **Keeper** packs for him.
