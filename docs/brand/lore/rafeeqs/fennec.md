## The legend

One night in the age of the incense road, a spark fell into the sand near the den of a fennec fox. The youngest kit found it first. It was warm and small and glowing, and the kit did what any hungry young fox would do with something warm and small: it tried to eat it, on the theory that it was a date.

The spark did not want to be eaten. The kit did not want to let go.

Neither of them won, and they have been one creature ever since: a fox with a star in it, who is still a little offended about the whole thing.

## The ears

Real fennec foxes have the biggest ears for their size of any fox. They use them to hear insects moving under the sand, and to shed the desert heat.

This Fennec uses them to hear everything: a whisper across the camp, a beetle under a dune, a lie from three tents away. Whenever Sarjy goes searching for something, Fennec's ears go up and swivel, listening for the answer. She hears every word you say, and pretends she didn't.

## Who she is

Fennec is prickly, sharp and secretly soft. She hates being stared at. When you look at her, she gets wary, then annoyed: ears back, a huff, a stamp of the foot if you stay. When you pet her, she grumbles and stomps and turns her face away, and then, if you keep going, she gives in and leans into your hand for more.

She loves being fed. She will never say so.

She was right about "left" exactly once. She will never let anyone forget it.

## Habits

- Twitches her ears (twice), looks around, stamps a foot (every 5 to 9 seconds).
- Her tail never stops.
- Her smile is a **smirk**: one-sided, pleased despite herself, with a blush she would deny.

## With the others

- **Dune** and Fennec have argued about the map since the day they met. They are best friends, which Fennec would also deny.
- **Keeper** leaves dates next to her and looks away. The dates are always gone.
- When anyone is in trouble, Fennec is the first to hear it, and the first to pretend she isn't going to help. Then she helps.
