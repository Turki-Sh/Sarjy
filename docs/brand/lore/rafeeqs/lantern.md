## The legend

Before roads had signposts, caravans that crossed the desert at night followed a **dalil**, a guide who could read the stars. One guide carried a small brass fanous to read his own hands by. One night, in the age of the incense road, a spark fell out of the Straw Road and straight into it.

The fanous didn't go out after that. It glowed a soft violet, the color of the sky just after sunset, and it stayed lit through wind and rain, all the way to morning.

Lantern walked beside that guide for the rest of his life, and then beside his son, and then his son's daughter. For a hundred years she lit the way for night travellers. In all that time she never once let a caravan get lost, and she never forgot a single thing anyone told her on the road.

In Japan they say a thing that has served faithfully for a hundred years wakes up with a soul of its own. Lantern, when she heard this, said it seemed very reasonable.

## The light that remembers

Lantern's light is memory. Every time you trust Sarjy with something, Lantern's fanous lights, because one more small thing is now being kept, and keeping things is what light is for on a dark road. The color of that light, Dusk, is the color of the hour when travellers start telling each other things.

She knows every star by its old Arabic name, and she is the one who taught the others the stories of the sky: Suhail and his waterskin, the daughters of Na'sh, the follower who walks behind al-Thurayya. She tells them slowly, by the fire, and she never tells the same one quite the same way twice.

The little curl on top of her head is the very first flame she ever caught.

## Who she is

Lantern is the calm one and the wise one. She speaks softly. When you look at her, she glows warmly at you. When you pet her, she melts. When something goes wrong, she worries, quietly. Her eyes are sleepy-soft at rest, and they open when you talk to her.

She keeps watch late into the night. Of everyone, she is the one who most believes that not everyone who wanders is lost.

## Habits

- Tilts her head, yawns, looks around (slowly, every 10 to 16 seconds).
- Her fanous sways and flickers, always.
- Her face at rest is **content**.
- Her smile is **serene**: eyes closed, a gentle curve.
- On a save, she doesn't bounce. She glows.

## With the others

- **Rider** and Lantern are the two who can find the way home, Rider by the wells and Lantern by the stars.
- **Breeze** keeps trying to blow her light out. It never works. They both find this funny.
- Every one of them, sooner or later, has fallen asleep with their head against Lantern's glow.
