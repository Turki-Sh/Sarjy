## The legend

On the Night of Iron a caravan had stopped to sleep in the Empty Quarter. The camels knelt in a ring and the riders slept beside them, saddles still on. When the sky split and the iron came down, one spark fell straight into an empty saddle.

The camel under it woke, stood up, and found it had a rider.

That is the whole of Rider's origin, and he tells it in exactly that many words. The saddle on his back is the one he was born with. He has never taken it off, and he never will, because the saddle is not something Rider wears. It is where Rider comes from.

Rider walked with the caravans from Najran to the Hijaz, along the old incense road, and learned the wells one by one: their names, their depths, which ones run sweet and which ones run salt, which ones the caravans stopped trusting a hundred years ago and why. He learned them the way you learn the names of your family. Ask Rider about any well between the Yemen and the Hijaz and he will tell you its name before you finish the question. (Ask him about a well he doesn't know, and he will look troubled for a long moment and say: *"Every well has a name. Not this one."*)

The brass medallion on his strap was struck from a sliver of the iron that fell with him. On cold nights it is the only warm metal in the camp.

## Who he is

Rider is the steady one. Nothing startles him. When the others panic, Rider is already standing, already facing the right way, already saying *"Stay close."* When you look at him, he stands to attention with a small nod. When you pet him, he takes it with dignity: a proud nod, no fuss. When something goes wrong, he doesn't droop. He sets his jaw and tries again.

He is the Rafeeq closest to Sarjy itself. Sarjy means *my saddle*, and Rider is the only Rafeeq that carries one. The others like to say Rider is half Rafeeq and half Sarjy. Rider neither confirms nor denies this.

His one weakness is that he is so certain of the road that he never thinks to ask which way the sun comes up. *"We ride at dawn,"* he announces, every time. Then, quietly: *"Which way is dawn?"*

## Habits

- Looks around, nods, twitches an ear, tilts his head (every 7 to 12 seconds, like a sentry).
- Stays awake longer than almost anyone (he dozes only after a long quiet).
- His smile is **proud**: neat, closed, satisfied.

## With the others

- **Drifter** sleeps in his saddle on long walks. Rider pretends to mind.
- **Lantern** and Rider are the only two who can find the way home: Lantern by the stars, Rider by the wells. When they disagree, they are both right, and the others have to wait while they work out how.
- **Dune** keeps trying to lead. Rider lets him, for exactly one dune.
