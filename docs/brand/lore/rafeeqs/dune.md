## The legend

Long before the Night of Iron, when the incense caravans were still finding their way north, a spark came down on the tallest dune in the Empty Quarter and landed right on the crest.

The dune woke up.

A whole dune can't go anywhere, of course. So the part of it that woke, the part with the spark in it, slid down the slip face, shook itself off, and walked away to see the world, leaving the rest of the dune behind to keep its place. That is why Dune insists he is the tallest dune in the Empty Quarter (he is, in a sense) and why he is also, standing up very straight, about knee-high.

On his first morning, the wind brought him a red shemagh that a traveller had lost, and an agal to hold it in place. Dune put them on, looked at his shadow on the sand, and decided he was a king. He has worn them like a crown ever since.

## The singing dunes

In a few places in Arabia the sand hums and booms when it slides down a dune, a low sound you can hear a long way off. Scientists call it booming sand.

Dune will tell you this is laughing, and that the dunes are laughing at something Dune said.

## Who he is

Dune is bold, cheerful and a show-off. He loves an audience. When you look at him, he puffs up, chin high. When you pet him, he giggles and bounces. When something goes wrong, he is indignant. He is never, ever the one who was wrong.

He laughs loud, sulks for a second, and laughs again. He is impossible to stay annoyed with, which he knows, and uses.

## Habits

- Hops, nods, tilts his head (every 6 to 10 seconds).
- His shemagh flutters a little in the wind, always.
- His smile is a **laugh**: wide open, eyes squeezed shut.

## With the others

- **Fennec** and Dune have argued about the map since the day they met. They are best friends.
- Everyone follows Dune when they're lost, because he sounds the most sure. This is usually a mistake.
- **Rider** lets Dune lead for exactly one dune.
- In a pinch, Dune is the tallest thing for miles. That turns out to matter.
